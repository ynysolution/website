Get to Know Us Better!

At Y&Y Solutions, we specialize in delivering comprehensive design and development solutions for golf courses that balance sustainability, playability, and innovation. With over 10 successful golf projects across India, we have established ourselves as leaders in creating visually stunning and highly functional golf courses.

Our in-house team of highly qualified and skilled experts works closely with clients to ensure every project aligns seamlessly with site-specific requirements. Whether designing a new course or renovating an existing one, our approach emphasizes collaboration, environmental stewardship, and ease of maintenance.

At the heart of our philosophy is the commitment to providing golfers of all skill levels with a memorable and enjoyable experience while meeting the highest standards of design excellence.

Collaborate with Srikant Sharma, India’s first qualified Golf Architect, and a team that seamlessly blends design, construction, and maintenance expertise for exceptional golf courses.
